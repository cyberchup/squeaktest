About

Estimates whether the speech in a recording is synthetic (AI-generated or voice-cloned), and shows which parts look suspicious. Built for checking a suspicious voicemail, voice note or call recording.

Your audio is checked, analyzed and deleted straight away. It is never stored, and nothing about it is logged except its size, length, format and result band.

Know its limits

  • The score is the model's raw output, not a probability. Act on the band, and verify through another channel (call back on a known number).
  • Opus voice notes and VoIP audio cause more false alarms than other audio.
  • Tones, beeps and music can score as synthetic.
  • Tested on English speech only, and on deepfakes from 2022 and earlier. Newer deepfakes are probably harder to catch.
